# GlobalTech Innovations — Remote Work & Employee Handbook Policy

**Document ID:** GT-POL-2026-V3  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Audience:** All Full-Time and Contract Employees  

---

## 1. Remote Work Eligibility & Core Working Hours
All engineering, product, and design personnel are eligible for 100% remote work across recognized operational time zones. Employees must maintain availability during our core synchronization window: **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Time (ET)**, Monday through Thursday. Fridays are designated as "Deep Focus Days" with no scheduled internal meetings after 12:00 PM.

## 2. Home Office Setup Allowance & Equipment
- **Initial Stipend:** Every newly hired full-time team member receives a one-time reimbursement of **$1,500 USD** within their first 60 days to equip an ergonomic home workstation (monitor, desk, chair).
- **Hardware Provided:** The company provisions an Apple MacBook Pro (M3 Max, 36GB RAM) or a Dell XPS 15 (64GB RAM, Ubuntu Linux) along with hardware YubiKeys for multi-factor authentication.
- **Monthly Connectivity Subsidy:** An automatic reimbursement of **$100 USD per month** is credited to payroll to cover high-speed fiber internet and mobile communication expenses.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Entitlements
1. **Flexible Vacation:** GlobalTech operates a flexible paid-time-off policy with a recommended minimum of **20 days per calendar year**.
2. **Sick & Mental Health Days:** 10 dedicated wellness days provided annually, requiring no physician note for absences under 3 consecutive business days.
3. **Parental Leave:** 16 consecutive weeks of 100% paid leave for primary caregivers and 8 weeks for secondary caregivers, applicable to both biological and adopted children.
4. **Bereavement Leave:** Up to 10 paid business days for immediate family members.

## 4. Professional Development & Learning Budget
Each employee is granted an annual learning stipend of **$2,500 USD**. Eligible expenses include:
- Technical certifications (AWS, GCP, CKA, MongoDB Certified Professional).
- Industry conferences and workshops (including travel and lodging expenses).
- Academic coursework, books, and software subscriptions related to career progression.

## 5. Security Protocols for Remote Workers
- **VPN Requirement:** Access to production infrastructure, staging databases, and internal dashboards must route through the enterprise Tailscale zero-trust VPN.
- **Password Management:** 1Password enterprise vaults are mandatory. Sharing plain-text credentials over Slack or email is strictly prohibited.
- **Data Protection:** No customer identifiable data (PII) may be exported to local developer machines without automated synthetic masking.
